外籍人才政策与工作签证路径

Expatriate Policy & Work Visa Pathway

  • Employment Visa(工作签证):雇主担保,2年可续签,费用约3,000 AED,审批5天
  • Golden Visa(黄金签证):高技能人才10年长期居留,无需雇主担保,费用5,000 AED,审批15天
  • Green Visa(绿色签证):自雇/技能工作者5年居留,无需雇主担保,2,500 AED,审批10天
  • Freelancer Visa(自由职业):DIC/DMCC自由区1年可续签,1,500 AED,审批7天
  • 推荐路径:先拿Employment Visa入职 → 2年后转Golden Visa(长期居留+独立续签能力)
  • ⚡ 阿联酋无永住/入籍路径(极少数特殊贡献除外),但Golden Visa是等效的长期居留方案

  • Phase 3 (Post-arrival): Medical fitness + Emirates ID biometrics + Labour Card = 8-15 days in-country
  • Phase 4 (Settlement): Bank account + insurance registration = 7-14 days — mandatory for salary via WPS
  • Total visa cost to employer: ~USD 2,500-4,000 (varies by free zone); candidate out-of-pocket: ~USD 685
  • Critical path risk: WES evaluation delays (non-accredited degrees) and medical fitness failure (HIV/TB positive = deportation)

  • UAE职场文化核心特征：关系驱动型、层级分明、遵循伊斯兰日历，wasta（人脉网络）对职业发展至关重要
